1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Outside the Box ("we", "our", or "the game") processes information when you use the Outside the Box mobile game, its online services, and this website.

We process only the information needed to operate the game, authenticate players, save progress, provide leaderboards, maintain security, understand performance, and support advertising where advertising is enabled.

2. Information we process

Account and authentication information

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides an authorization credential and platform player identifier to Unity Authentication. We do not receive your Google password. If you continue as a guest, Unity Authentication creates an anonymous player identifier and stores a session token on your device.

Game and cloud progress

We process your player identifier, completed levels, unlocked chapters, scores, stars, virtual currency, settings, tutorial status, suspended runs, leaderboard results, and similar gameplay progress. This information is associated with your Unity player profile and stored using Unity Cloud Save.

Device, diagnostics, and analytics information

Depending on the game version and your choices, our service providers may process device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate region, network status, crash information, performance information, gameplay events, and pseudonymous identifiers.

Advertising information

When advertising is enabled, advertising and mediation providers may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device information, consent choices, ad interactions, and fraud-prevention signals. Personalized advertising is used only where permitted and based on the consent choices presented to you.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate players and maintain secure sessions.
  • Save, restore, and synchronize game progress.
  • Operate campaign and endless leaderboards.
  • Provide rewards, purchases, settings, and game features.
  • Diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve performance.
  • Measure how the game is used and improve level balance.
  • Display and measure advertising when ads are enabled.
  • Meet legal, safety, fraud-prevention, and platform requirements.

Google user data obtained through Google Play Games is used only to authenticate your account, link it to your game profile, and provide the game features described here. We do not sell Google user data.

4. Service providers

The game may use the following providers. Their handling of information is also governed by their own policies:

  • Google Play Games Services for platform authentication and player services.
  • Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, and Leaderboards for player identity, progress, and scores.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Remote Config for analytics and remotely managed game configuration.
  • Unity LevelPlay and mediated advertising networks, which may include ironSource, Google AdMob, and Unity Ads, for rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertising where enabled.

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Providers use contractual, organizational, and technical protections appropriate to their services and applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We retain account and game-progress information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the game, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Analytics and advertising providers apply their own retention periods.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can control music, sound, vibration, accessibility options, and—where available—advertising consent from within the game. Device settings may allow you to reset or limit the advertising identifier.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To request access or deletion of your game profile, contact us using the address below and include your Unity Player ID if available. Deleting the app alone does not necessarily delete cloud data. An anonymous guest account may be unrecoverable after uninstalling unless it was linked to Google Play Games.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children below the minimum digital-consent age applicable in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children contrary to applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice when required.
